## Persistence / duplicate policy

Schema version 5 adds the October inventory automatically once to an older local store. A prior item with the exact same name, slot, base stats and enchantment is recognised, enriched with missing condition/mutation and not duplicated. Distinct rolls or authenticating histories are **not automatically replaced or deleted**. A prior item entered with +7.7% NPC Offers represented as a Normal authentication versus the new Race Valk's base stat will remain separate unless manually reconciled; merging those would require certainty they are the same physical item. Deleting gear after the migration will not trigger reimport on the next launch.

No historical gear, trophy, vehicle, certificate roll, or settings data should be discarded by this migration. Export a JSON backup before production upgrade.
